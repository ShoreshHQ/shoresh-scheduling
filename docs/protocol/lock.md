# LOCK protocol: locks and hand edits

What a person decides beats what Shoresh generates. A **lock** (CORE-1.22) is a person's mark that
a cell stays when the week is built again; a **hand edit** (CORE-1.23) is a placement a person
made. A hand edit is always allowed, never refused for a setup reason, and shown with a reminder
(Greg, 2026-10-26); on a generated week it becomes a lock. Locks are per cell (2026-10-17).

LOCK uses CORE layer 1 (1.22, 1.23), layer 4 (CORE-4.8: a hand edit may break a limit and is then
shown), layer 5 (CORE-5.1: locks and hand edits are laid down first and never moved) and layer 6
(CORE-6.1). GEN obeys it. It never redefines a CORE term. IDs, tests and the index are as in
[README.md](README.md).

## Already stated by GEN

- GEN-2.3: a full build clears every activity not locked; a lock on any cell of a multi-block
  activity keeps all of it.
- GEN-2.5: a locked Open cell stays Open.
- GEN-8.13, 10.8: locks and hand edits never move, in the search or a Rebuild.
- GEN-9.9: a hand edit is never refused for a closure, a group away or not at camp, or a fixed or
  recurring activity moved, covered or cleared; it is saved, locked on Generated, with a reminder.
